Preface

This little book was not typeset by hand. It was written as one long HTML document and cut into pages by a script that measures every paragraph in the browser, the way a word processor measures lines, and then decides where each page ends.

Everything you see here comes from that one pass: the roman numerals in front, the chapters that always open on a right-hand page, the running headers, the footnotes at the foot of the page that cites them, and the index at the back, whose page numbers were filled in once the pages existed.
